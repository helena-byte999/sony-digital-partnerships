06 — Why me

The role, line
by line.

Taken straight from the job description, and what I bring to each.

What the role needs
What I bring
Weekly pitching of songs and albums to DSPs
I already pitch music for a living, from the booth. Every set is a run of decisions about which record deserves to go next and what it will do to the room, and I make those calls live, most weeks of the year. Pitching a track to a DSP is the same argument written down: this song, this audience, this reason. I have simply been making it out loud, to a crowd that answers immediately.
Monitor release info and metadata; artist profile optimisation
I am a software engineer, and I have built recommender systems end to end. I know precisely how metadata quality decides what a platform can surface and what a profile has to look like for an algorithm to trust it. I also see the other side of that every week: when I dig for tracks, the records with thin or messy data are the ones nobody ever finds. I have been on both ends of that problem, as the person building the system and the person hunting through it.
Create DSP marketing assets for labels
Brand identity for Snap Lens Lab, marketing collateral at Aberdeen Commercial Group, lead designer at Epsilon Labs, and the promo I wrote and directed for my own show. Designing decks, artwork and ad creative is something I do by default, so DSP assets are familiar ground rather than a new skill to pick up.
Team admin, minutes, mailing lists, release schedule, ticket requests
Event production is this job under a harder deadline. I have run the schedules, sent the email blasts ahead of promotion, built the playlists that set the room, briefed vendors, designed the stage and handled ticketing and guest lists. The doors open at a fixed time, so nothing is allowed to slip.
Build relationships with multiple stakeholders
DJing is a relationship business. I have built a name across Abuja and Lagos by turning up, playing well and staying in touch with the promoters, founders and artists I meet along the way. Inside teams I have worked with engineers, designers, product people and vendors on the same project, and I am usually the one translating between them.
Tenacious, motivated, curious
I taught myself to DJ at ten years old. Fourteen years later I am still doing it, and still curious about how far it goes. I am not looking to stop at an internship either. I want to do work at Sony that reaches the communities the catalogue has not reached yet, and I intend to still be building long after the placement ends.
London, 3 to 4 days in office
Relocating to London and available in the office full time.
